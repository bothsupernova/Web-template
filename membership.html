Membership Terms

Monthly billing.
Your first payment is charged when you join. Your membership renews automatically at $110 per month for the 60 minute plan or $165 per month for the 90 minute plan until you cancel. There is no minimum commitment.
Unused sessions.
While your membership is active, you can carry one unused massage into the next billing cycle. Use it by the end of that cycle. Its complimentary add-on carries over with it.
Membership cancellation.
Cancel online before your next billing date to stop the next charge. You can use any remaining benefits through the end of your current paid billing cycle.
Personal use and promotions.
Membership benefits are for the enrolled member. Gratuity is separate. Membership savings cannot be combined with other promotions.
Appointment changes.
Please give at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment. A late cancellation or missed appointment may count as your included session.
